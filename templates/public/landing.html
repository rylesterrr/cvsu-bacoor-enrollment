About the campus

Quality education, closer to home.

Established in 2008, Cavite State University – Bacoor City Campus provides quality and affordable education to the people of Bacoor and nearby communities. The campus offers undergraduate programs in information technology, computer science, business, psychology, criminology, hospitality management, and teacher education.
